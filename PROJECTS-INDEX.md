## Status vocabulary

-   Concept: idea only.
-   Prototype: a basic version exists.
-   Working: tested on a real device.
-   Production-ready: security, reliability, tests, documentation, and
    deployment have been verified.

Before sharing or selling any project, update its status honestly, check
third-party licenses and asset rights, remove secrets, and confirm what
rights can legally be transferred.
