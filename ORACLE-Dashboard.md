# ORACLE --- Scenario and Timeline Analysis Dashboard

## Concept

A dashboard for exploring possible project timelines, assumptions,
uncertainty, and alternative scenarios. It is a planning tool, not a
supernatural prediction engine or guaranteed forecast.

## Proposed modules

-   Monte Carlo scenario simulations when inputs and distributions are
    specified.
-   Timeline convergence visualization.
-   Assumption and uncertainty indicators.
-   Scenario comparison and sensitivity analysis.
-   Agent-style modules named Alpha, Beta, and Gamma for separate
    analyses.
-   Exportable reports with assumptions and limitations.

## Technical requirements

-   Define inputs, distributions, constraints, and objective.
-   Show how results were calculated.
-   Include uncertainty ranges and explain that outputs depend on
    assumptions.
-   Test calculations with known examples.
-   Do not label a "breakthrough year" as certain; present it as a
    scenario.
